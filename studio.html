About Mōno™

(©23)

Every product begins with an idea. We explore shape, curious perspectives, and find the balance between creativity and practical utility. ASA is a creative studio focused on developing original lighting objects inspired by Vietnamese heritage and everyday life.

(©24)

For us, a product is more than something visually appealing. It is a way to express an idea, tell a story and evoke an emotion. From initial concepts to finished objects, we explore form, material, and function to create pieces that bring personality into everyday spaces.

(©25)

Every NOLA lamp is precision 3D-printed from sustainable PLA — plant starch derived from corn and sugarcane. Each component is sanded, wired, and assembled by hand in our studio in Saigon, ensuring tactile warmth and enduring quality.

(©26)

Attention to detail defines everything we do — from personalized name engraving and signature charms to electrical safety and recyclable packaging. Creating objects that bring character into everyday spaces. Future in form.
